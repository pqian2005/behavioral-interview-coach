# Behavioral Interview Coach

A Claude Code skill for preparing senior-level behavioral interview answers. Built by Ping Q — a 20-year fintech veteran who used this system to land Sr PM and Senior BA roles at top tech companies.

## What It Does

This skill turns Claude into an expert behavioral interview coach that helps you:

- Extract strong stories from your raw experience
- Structure answers using the **5-Act Story Flow** framework
- Transform factual answers into impressive, senior-leader delivery
- Practice under simulated interview conditions
- Adapt one story to multiple question angles and competencies

Works for Sr PM, Senior BA, Principal, Director, and equivalent roles at any company.

## Usage

Once installed, the skill activates automatically when you mention anything related to interview prep. You can also trigger it explicitly:

- "Help me practice a behavioral interview question"
- "I have an interview coming up — can you coach me?"
- "Turn this story into an impressive answer"
- "Mock interview — leadership question"
- "How do I answer LP questions?"

## Core Framework: The 5-Act Story Flow

Every answer follows this structure:

| Act | Duration | Purpose |
|-----|----------|---------|
| Act 1 — Context | 5 sec | Establish who you are and what you owned |
| Act 2 — The Problem | 15 sec | Create tension. Be honest and sharp. |
| Act 3 — Stakes | 10 sec | **The impression moment.** Why did this matter to the business? |
| Act 4 — Actions | 30 sec | Three distinct actions mapping to different skills |
| Act 5 — Outcome | 10 sec | Short, declarative, complete |

> **The Golden Rule:** Facts tell. Stakes sell. Ownership closes.

## Session Formats

**Full Practice Session** — Cold delivery → Feedback → Model answer → 3 practice passes

**Story Generator** — Share a raw story and get a fully structured, impressive version back

## What Makes It Different

Most interview prep focuses on structure. This skill focuses on *impression* — specifically the language upgrades and stakes framing that cause interviewers to look up from their notes. It also includes:

- LP/competency framework mapping
- Delivery coaching checklist
- Common failure patterns and fixes
- Practice protocol (minimum 5 passes before a story is "ready")
